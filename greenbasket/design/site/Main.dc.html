See all FAQs
Where do you deliver?

Across Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. Enter your PIN code at the top of this page to check your area before you shop.

How do delivery slots work?

You pick a slot at checkout. Each slot takes a set number of orders and closes when it is full. We hold your slot while you pay.

What if an item is short or damaged?

We never swap an item without asking you. If something is short we refund it, and you can report damaged items from your account for review.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, where eligible. Checkout shows whether cash on delivery is available for your order and area.
