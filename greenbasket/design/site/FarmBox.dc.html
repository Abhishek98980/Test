Farm Box questions

Is there a minimum for a box?

No. A box can be one bunch of coriander. Your whole order still needs to reach ₹199, and delivery is free from ₹499.

Can I change a saved box?

Yes. Choose Edit in builder, change anything, and save it with the same name to replace it, or a new name to keep both.

What if something in my box is out of stock?

We leave it out and you aren’t charged for it. We never swap an item without asking you first.

Can a box arrive every week by itself?

Yes, through a subscription. Pick a plan and fill it with the same items each week.
