About this product

Description

Everyday hybrid tomatoes, picked ripe and firm so they hold up in curries and keep two or three days on the counter. Sold in fixed packs: the price you see is the price you pay.

Storage and freshness

Keep at room temperature, stem side down, out of direct sun. Refrigerate only once fully ripe, and use within 2 days of refrigerating.

Pack and weight

Each pack is weighed before it leaves us and is never lighter than the weight on the label. Count varies with size, usually 8 to 10 tomatoes per kilo.
