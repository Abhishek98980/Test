Wholesale questions

Do I need a business to buy wholesale?

No. Anyone can buy at wholesale rates once an order reaches a tier. A GSTIN only matters if you want it on your invoice.

Can I mix wholesale and store items in one order?

Yes. They arrive together. Coupons apply to store items only.

Is there a minimum wholesale order?

The tier is the minimum for its rate. The whole order needs to reach [WHOLESALE MINIMUM] for the morning wholesale run.

Can I get monthly credit?

Regular buyers can apply after [CREDIT ELIGIBILITY]. Choose “Monthly credit” in the quote form and we’ll talk you through it.
