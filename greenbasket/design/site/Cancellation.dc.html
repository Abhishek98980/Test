Cancelling an order

You can cancel any order from My account until we start packing it, about 2 hours before your slot. You get a full refund, including any delivery fee.

Once packing has started: [CANCELLATION AFTER PACKING].

Cash on delivery orders can be cancelled the same way. Nothing has been charged, so there’s nothing to refund.

When we cancel

We may cancel an order, in full or in part, if a payment fails, if your address turns out to be outside our area, or if items aren’t available. We’ll tell you on WhatsApp or SMS and refund anything you paid.

Short, damaged or not fresh items

Please check your order when it arrives. If something is missing, weighs less than the pack says, is damaged, or isn’t fresh, report it within [REPORT WINDOW] of delivery:

  • Open the order in My account and choose Report a problem.
  • Tick the items and say what went wrong.
  • Add photos for damaged or stale items. They help us sort it faster.
  • Choose a refund, store credit or a free replacement.

A person on our support team reviews every report and replies within [RESPONSE TIME].

Replacements

A replacement comes free with your next delivery. If you’d like it sooner: [SAME-DAY REPLACEMENT RULE].

We never swap an item for a different one without asking you first.

How refunds are paid

  • UPI, card or net banking: back to the same account, usually in 5 to 7 working days.
  • Store credit: added to your account the same day, if you choose it.
  • Cash on delivery: [COD REFUND METHOD].
  • Bank transfer: back to the sending account within [BANK REFUND TIME].

How long your bank takes to show the money is up to your bank.

Missed deliveries

If our rider can’t reach you, the order comes back to the store. You can pick a new slot for free from My account. For prepaid orders: [MISSED DELIVERY POLICY].

Subscriptions

Skip any week, or pause for one, two or four weeks, before that week’s cut-off at 10 PM the night before delivery. You aren’t charged for skipped or paused weeks.

Cancel from My account at any time. A delivery already past its cut-off still arrives and is charged. Notice and refund terms: [SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION TERMS].

Wholesale orders

Please weigh and check wholesale orders when they arrive, and report problems within [REPORT WINDOW]. The same refund and replacement options apply. Orders on monthly credit are adjusted on your next statement.

Returns

Fresh produce can’t be resold, so we don’t take items back for a change of mind: [CHANGE OF MIND POLICY]. If anything is wrong with your order, report it and we’ll put it right.
