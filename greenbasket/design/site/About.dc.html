About Greeen Basket

Fresh produce for the Tricity, packed the morning it arrives.

We’re a Chandigarh team selling fruit and vegetables online to homes, kitchens and shops across Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. We pack every order ourselves and deliver it with our own riders.

Why we started

Buying good produce shouldn’t mean an early trip to the mandi, or a delivery where half the tomatoes are soft and the coriander has been swapped for something else.

So we built a shop that works the way we wanted to buy: clear prices on every pack, slots that don’t get overbooked, and nothing changed without asking you first.

What we promise

01

The price on the pack is what you pay.

Every pack shows its weight and its price per kilo or piece. No weighing at the door, no surprise top-ups.

02

Nothing swapped without asking.

If an item runs short, we refund it or call you first. Your choice, set at checkout.

03

Slots that hold.

Each slot takes a set number of orders and closes when it’s full, so your delivery isn’t squeezed in late.

04

A person reads every report.

Short, damaged or not fresh? Tell us from your account. Our support team reviews every request, with photos.
