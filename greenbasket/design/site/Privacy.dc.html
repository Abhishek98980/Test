1. Who we are

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME], which runs Greeen Basket, decides how your personal data is used. Under the law we are the “data fiduciary”. Registered address: [REGISTERED ADDRESS].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number and, if you add it, your email.
  • Delivery details: addresses, PIN codes and delivery notes. We don’t ask for your phone’s location.
  • Orders: what you buy, when, your slots, subscriptions and saved boxes.
  • Payments: our payment partner handles card and bank details. We receive the payment status, method and the last 4 digits of a card.
  • Business details, if you give them: business name and GSTIN.
  • What you send us: messages, reviews, and photos of order problems.
  • Site use: essential cookies, and analytics only if you agree.

3. Why we use it

  • To take, pack and deliver your orders, and tell you about them.
  • To take payment, issue invoices and keep records the law requires.
  • To handle refunds, replacements and questions.
  • To run subscriptions, including reminders before each cut-off.
  • To prevent fraud, such as fake cash-on-delivery orders.
  • With your consent: to understand how the site is used, and to send offers.

4. Your consent

We ask for consent in plain words, separately for each optional purpose, like offers on WhatsApp or analytics cookies. You can withdraw it at any time in My account or the cookie settings below, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing doesn’t affect what we did before.

5. Who we share it with

  • Our riders, who see your name, number, address and note for your delivery.
  • Payment partner: [GATEWAY NAME].
  • Messaging: [MESSAGING PROVIDER] for WhatsApp and SMS.
  • Hosting: [HOSTING PROVIDER].
  • Analytics, only if you agree: [ANALYTICS PROVIDER].
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

They may use your data only to provide their service to us. We don’t sell your data.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account details: until you delete your account, plus [GRACE PERIOD] in case you change your mind.
  • Orders and invoices: [RETENTION PERIOD], as tax law requires.
  • Photos of order problems: [PHOTO RETENTION].

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold and who we’ve shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update it.
  • Erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Let someone you nominate act for you if you die or can’t act yourself.

Most of this is in My account, under Privacy and your data. Or write to our grievance officer. We reply within [RIGHTS RESPONSE TIME]. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

Greeen Basket is for adults. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 without a parent’s verifiable consent.

9. Keeping it safe

We encrypt data in transit, limit who on our team can see it, and review access regularly. If a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Cookies

Essential cookies keep you signed in and remember your basket. They can’t be turned off. Analytics cookies help us see which pages work, and only run if you agree. Advertising cookies: [NONE, OR LIST THEM].
