Subscription questions

What is the allowance?

It’s how much produce, at store prices, you can put in each delivery. It’s always more than the plan price, so the difference is your saving.

What if my basket goes over?

We add the extra at store prices to that week’s charge. You’ll see the amount before the cut-off.

What if I don’t use all of it?

Unused allowance [ROLLOVER RULE]. We’ll remind you the day before the cut-off so it isn’t wasted.

Can I skip a week or pause?

Yes. Skip any week before its cut-off, or pause for one, two or four weeks from My account. You aren’t charged for skipped or paused weeks.

How do I cancel?

From My account, with one button and a confirmation. A delivery already past its cut-off still arrives. See the cancellation policy.
